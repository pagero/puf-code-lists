=== Allowance reason codes Philippines
The invoice must show the discount granted to a senior citizen, person with disability, national athlete or coach, solo parent or Medal of Valor awardee or dependent, together with the buyer's ID number (RR No. 7-2024, Section 6(B)(18)). For the ID number see https://pagero.github.io/puf-code-lists/#_identification_scheme_philippines[Identification scheme Philippines^].

|===
|Value |Description

|SC | Senior Citizen Discount.
|PWD | Person With Disability Discount
|NAAC | National Athletes and Coaches Discount.
|SOLO_PARENT | Solo Parent Discount.
|MOV | Medal of Valor Discount, for the awardee or a dependent.
