=== Tax Exemption Codes in Oman

Oman requires a VAT exemption reason code whenever the VAT category code is `E` (Exempt from tax) or `Z` (Zero rated). Provide the reason code at the same level as the category code it explains, that is on the VAT breakdown, on the invoice line, or on a document level allowance or charge.

The two sets are separate and MUST NOT be mixed. `VATEX-OM-*` applies to category `E` only, and `VATZR-OM-*` to category `Z` only.

|Value |Description

|`VATEX-OM-01`
|Qualifying Financial Services.

|`VATEX-OM-02`
|Educational Services and Related Goods and Services.

|`VATEX-OM-03`
|Healthcare Services and related Goods and Services.

|`VATEX-OM-04`
|Rental of properties for residential purposes.

|`VATEX-OM-05`
|Local Passenger Transport.

|`VATEX-OM-06`
|Undeveloped land (bare land).

|`VATEX-OM-09`
|Imports for the Armed Forces and Internal Security Forces.

|`VATEX-OM-10`
|Imported supplies for non-profit charities.

|`VATEX-OM-11`

|`VATEX-OM-12`
